## Latency (d)

Method: `addInitScript` wraps `EventSource` (every SSE message stamped with `Date.now()` on arrival) and a `MutationObserver`
stamps every change of the visible attempt count and of the "Đã dùng chung" value. Backend time: block timestamp (`cast block -f timestamp`, 1 s resolution,
floored, so a real latency is up to 1 s lower) and the attempt `ts`. "DOM" is the row-count change on screen.

| # | Event | DOM minus block ts | DOM minus attempt ts | SSE arrival minus block ts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | step 0 swap, block 966 | 3.00 s | 2.00 s | 2.99 s |
| 2 | step 1 revert, block 967 | 2.42 s | 1.42 s | 2.42 s |
| 3 | step 2 revert, block 968 | 2.30 s | 1.30 s | 2.30 s |
| 4 | step 3 pay, block 969 | 3.02 s | 1.02 s | 3.02 s |
| 5 | step 4 pay, block 970 | 2.86 s | 1.86 s | 2.86 s |
| 6 | step 5 revert, block 971 | 3.19 s | 2.19 s | 3.19 s |
| 7 | lock, block 972 | 2.35 s | 2.35 s | 2.35 s |
| 8 | step 7 node reject (no block) | n/a | 2.10 s | n/a |
| 9 | Council approve, block 973 | not stamped | | 3.64 s |
| 10 | Council approve, block 974 | not stamped | | 4.25 s |
| 11 | #13 executed, block 975 | not stamped | | 2.73 s |

The screen follows SSE by 3-5 ms; the delay is chain to API. Against the attempt `ts` all 8 attempts are <= 2.35 s; against the block timestamp 3 of 7 are
3.0-3.2 s and the council events 2.7-4.3 s. Backend counter `/api/health` `eventToSse` after the walk: last 861 ms, max 2088 ms, 27 samples.
The API answered in 2-140 ms (was 5-26 s before #113).
